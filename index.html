Why Reducing Balance Matters

  • Fairer calculation: Interest is calculated only on the remaining balance you still owe, decreasing every month as you pay down principal.
  • Major savings: A ₦1,000,000 loan at 27% for 12 months costs ₦152,209 on reducing balance, compared to ₦270,000 if charged with a misleading flat rate.
  • Industry standard: This is the transparent method regulated for Nigerian commercial banks.

Market reference: Zenith MSME baseline (27% p.a., Nairametrics Nigerian Bank Lending Analysis). Access Bank "One Mama" baseline (20% interest + 1% management & 1% insurance charges, National Daily). Rates vary; confirm exact charges with your financial institution.
